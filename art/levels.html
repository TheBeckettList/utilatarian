The three laws

Every place is a level of the same game Josie's already is. Break one of these and the level does not ship.

2-for-1

A step added in the OS must remove at least two steps in real life. Every spec carries a step ledger.

Done is a state of the world

Things finish when the real thing happens: the stick comes back, the bag is handed over, the boat leaves. No Ready, Done or Submit buttons.

Score the place, never the person

Waits, stock and busy times per station and per day. No per-person timers, ranks or streaks, ever.

Build a level: photos, blocks, art pass

A level is assembled from preset blocks that take the shape of the real place, then reshaped by a local designer to how it feels. Josie's is the precedent: the neighbor's business on the lot and the bathrooms were cut, the tree and the beach got the room.

Reshape rules (the designer's guide)

  • Cut what nobody in the game tends: bathrooms, storage, parking, a neighbor's business. Keep it if a role works there (a tank room, a hostel's bathrooms for housekeeping).
  • Keep what you'd point at: the counter, the sign, the tree, the dock, the view.
  • Compress distance, keep order: walls move, adjacency and direction don't. The sea stays on its side.
  • Enlarge the soul, shrink the generic: the signature up to about 1.5x.
  • Three of anything, unless the count is the job (tanks, stock).
  • Never add what isn't there. A missing piece is a labeled stand-in.

Objects grow

Every object someone tends has a state ladder of four rungs at most. The drawing changes, not a label. Only the last rung is loud, and it pulses only when there is a deadline. Always an icon or a word too, never color alone.

Bubbles

All words on a map live in bubbles anchored to their object: a white circle on a ledge, the object inside, a small pill under it. Two words and a number at most, only for the role that can act, timed to the quiet moment, and gone by itself when the world changes.

✕ Free text on the map

Outlined names floating over the water read as a web page, not a place (today's Tripwrights region map).

✓ Anchored pill bubbles

The name sits in a pill on the island's pin; places live in their own color. "Soon" is a busy pill, not red text.

Anatomy

  • Ring: none when calm, amber when low, rose when it needs a person. Dashed while it is a question ("How's the coffee?").
  • Ledge: a darker circle 4px under the white one, like every Josie card.
  • Inside: the object itself, drawn with the kit, never an emoji.
  • Pill: two words and a number at most, with its mark: ✓ ok, ! low, !! alert, … busy. Here: twelve tanks needed by the 1:30 boat.
  • Pulse: only for a deadline. One loud thing on screen at a time.

2-for-1, made checkable

Every spec adds a step ledger. Reading is free; a tap that writes costs one step. A step moved to someone else still counts. Guest effort is only paid by guest savings.
