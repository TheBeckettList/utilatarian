The dive-shop palette

Josie's pink belongs to Josie. Every dive shop runs on this chrome: lagoon teal for the game, dive-flag red only for the one loud thing, white sand and black gear on the map. Each shop then wears its own accent on its own things (Paradise: teal-blue trim, grey tin roofs).

The shop is a level

Paradise drawn from the owner's photos: the dock to the shaded end platform, the boat alongside, the raised sand behind its seawall, the white gable building, the breezeway stairs, the grey-roof building with its porch, office and classroom. Upstairs: the walkway on the inside third, two dorms and a room. Interiors not yet seen are stand-ins.

Front desk

The POS, dive-shop shaped. Same bones as Josie's register: categories on the left, big tiles, the ticket on the right, one big pill to finish. The diver's card works like Josie's diet toggles: it greys out what the diver can't book yet and says why, never hides it. Sizes travel with the diver, so nobody asks "what size fins?" twice.

Objects grow

Every object a dive shop tends, as a ladder of four rungs or fewer. The drawing changes, not a label. Safety is never a game: the weather call is the captain's, shown, never scored.
