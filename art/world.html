Regions

The strip above the vessel. Real coastlines (rough traces), real ferry routes, density drawn from how busy each island really is: Utila a town on a bay, Roatán towns along its south shore, Guanaja a stilt town on a cay, Koh Samui a ring road lined with houses. Utila and Koh Tao are drawn about twice true size next to their neighbors so they can be tapped.

Islands

The island screen. At most five spots, like the map in a farming game: places that have joined are drawn as themselves in their own colors; real activity spots with no place yet are faded and say soon. More than five: the rest live in the sheet.

Paradise, as a level

The dive shop redrawn on Josie's board: warm floors, back walls, life in the corners, status bubbles where the work is. The hostel rooms move to their own level (reshape note in the spec).
